
HYPOTHESIS TESTING SUMMARY
=======================================

Business Question:
Do male and female customers differ in average sales?

Null Hypothesis (H0):
There is no difference in average customer sales.

Alternative Hypothesis (H1):
There is a difference in average customer sales.

Statistical Test:
Welch's Independent Two-Sample T-Test

Significance Level: 0.05

DATA SUMMARY
---------------------------------------
Male Customers: 468
Female Customers: 445
Customers Excluded (Inconsistent Gender): 34

Male Average Sales: Rs. 143,641.79
Female Average Sales: Rs. 138,779.51

Mean Difference (Male - Female): Rs. 4,862.28

STATISTICAL RESULTS
---------------------------------------
T-Statistic: 0.620937
P-Value: 0.53479654
Degrees of Freedom: 908.44

95% Confidence Interval:
Lower Bound: Rs. -10,505.79
Upper Bound: Rs. 20,230.35

DECISION
---------------------------------------
Fail to Reject the Null Hypothesis

BUSINESS CONCLUSION
---------------------------------------
There is insufficient statistical evidence of a difference in average customer sales between male and female groups.

Note:
This analysis identifies an association between gender
groups and average customer sales. It does not establish
that gender causes differences in spending.
